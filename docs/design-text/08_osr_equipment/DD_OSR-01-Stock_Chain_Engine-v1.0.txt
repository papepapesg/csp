 The system of record for equipment stock balances + movements across Wananchi's two-tier stock topology: central
 warehouse (one per operator) and contractor vans (one logical stock location per contractor). Every router, ONT, splitter, wall
 socket, and cable that enters or leaves any location creates a movement row; balances are derived projections. Owned at
 operator scope. References equipment_sku.sku_id from PLM-CFG-06 (this module does not define SKUs); references
 contractor_id from EM-02 (this module does not define contractors).

 v1.0 scope: quantity tracking only at the SKU level — no individual equipment instance / serial number tracking.
 Serialized instance tracking is deferred to a future DD (OSR-INSTANCE-01 or equivalent). Returns + RMA workflows in v1.0
 work at the SKU + qty level.



 1. What this module is
 When a vendor delivers 200 Huawei HG8245H5 ONTs to Wananchi's central warehouse in Nairobi, the system needs to
 know: this SKU now has +200 quantity at this location. When 20 of those move out to franchise contractor
 contractor_ke_nairobi_a's van, the system needs to know: -20 at central, +20 at the van. When the installer at the van
 installs one at Hon. Sarah Wanjiku's house, the system needs to know: -1 at the van, and the customer's WO is bound to the
 install event. When she returns the equipment 18 months later at disconnect, +1 to the van. Every one of these events is a


SOPHIX BSS Design Documents                                                                                                                  Page 3
 movement; every movement adjusts balances; the movement log is the truth and the balance is a cache.

 The two-tier topology — central warehouse + contractor vans — is intentional. Wananchi operates in markets where field
 operations rely heavily on contractor networks; each contractor needs visible stock that they can dispatch from without
 round-tripping through central. The operator (Wananchi central ops) sees both tiers; the contractor sees only their van.

 What this module does
   1. Maintain stock locations — operator-scoped, two-tier: one CENTRAL_WAREHOUSE per operator, plus N
     CONTRACTOR_VAN locations (one per contractor with active coverage in EM-02).

   2. Record stock movements — every quantity change is an immutable movement row with reason code, qty, source/target
     locations, references to the originating business object (vendor delivery #, WO ID, return ID, write-off form #).

   3. Project balances — stock_balance(sku_id, location_id, qty_on_hand) is a derived materialized projection
     of all movements, refreshed asynchronously after each commit (and on demand via API).

   4. Validate stock availability — at WO assignment time, callers ask "does contractor X's van have a Huawei HG8245H5 in
     stock?" Yes/no answer with current qty.

   5. Reserve stock for committed WOs — when a WO is finalized but not yet installed, the qty is "reserved" — visible in
     balance but unavailable for other WOs. Resolved on install (movement RECORDED) or WO cancellation (reservation
     released).

   6. Emit movement events — every balance-changing event publishes to Kafka for downstream consumers (BIL-01 deposit
     charge, ops dashboards, EM-02 contractor performance metrics).

   7. Support cycle counts + adjustments — periodic physical inventory reconciliation produces ADJUSTED movements with
     audit trail (who counted, when, why the variance).

 What this module does NOT do
   - Doesn't define SKUs. SKU catalog (brand, model, ownership semantics, fees, kind, compatibility) lives in PLM-CFG-06.
     OSR-01 references equipment_sku.sku_id as FK; never duplicates SKU metadata.

   - Doesn't track individual equipment instances. Serial number tracking, per-instance lifecycle (received → in-van →
     installed → returned → RMA → written-off), per-instance warranty, per-instance customer binding — all deferred to a
     future DD. v1.0 works at the (sku_id, location_id, qty) level only. If a customer returns a defective ONT, OSR-01 records
     +1 to the contractor van's balance for that SKU; which specific physical ONT was returned is not knowable in v1.0.

   - Doesn't define contractors. Contractor entity, scope per region, skills, availability slots, employed staff — all EM-02.
     OSR-01 references contractor_id from EM-02 to identify which van is which. v1.0 ships a minimal stub
     osr_contractor_stub(contractor_id PK, contractor_name) to satisfy FK references; retired when EM-02
     lands.

   - Doesn't manage procurement. Vendor relationships, purchase orders, vendor SLAs, lead times — out of scope. A
     separate Procurement DD (pending) owns those; OSR-01 receives "vendor X delivered N units of SKU Y on date Z" as
     input and records the movement.

   - Doesn't handle RMA / repair workflows. RMA process orchestration (sending failed equipment back to vendor, tracking
     repair status, receiving refurbished units back) is a future DD. v1.0 records the inbound "returned from customer"
     movement and the outbound "written-off" movement; the in-between is out of scope.

   - Doesn't track equipment-on-customer-premises. Once an installer completes installation, OSR-01's record is "the SKU
     left the van as INSTALLED with reference to WO X". Where the physical equipment sits between install and disconnect is
     not OSR-01's concern in v1.0; the customer's account in ILM-CFG-01 carries the installed-equipment summary via the
     WO trail. (When serialized tracking lands, equipment-on-customer-premises will be the INSTALLED instance state.)

   - Doesn't gate WO finalization. WO module decides whether to proceed with a WO based on the OSR-01 stock-availability
     response, but the gating logic + business rules live in WO module's Drools rules, not here. OSR-01 just answers "qty
     available" honestly.

 Regional flexibility
 Stock chain operations are similar across operators (receive, transfer, install, return, write-off) but the operational scale and
 reporting cadence differ:

   - WIK (Kenya) — high volume; 24 active contractor vans across Nairobi + Mombasa + Diani; cycle counts quarterly on
     central, monthly per van.



SOPHIX BSS Design Documents                                                                                                     Page 4
   - WUG (Uganda) — smaller scale; ~10 active vans; cycle counts quarterly across the board.

   - WTZ (Tanzania) — smaller still; cycle count cadence TBD per operator.

   - YASSN (Senegal) — Dakar-centric; ~6 active vans; cycle counts monthly per van.

 The schema accommodates all four with operator-scoped data + per-operator config for cycle-count thresholds. v1.0 ships

 Glossary
  Term                                                          Meaning

  Stock location                                                A logical place where stock sits. Two kinds: CENTRAL_WAREHOUSE (one per
                                                                operator) and CONTRACTOR_VAN (one per active contractor).

  Movement                                                      A single quantity change for one SKU at one location, with a reason code,
                                                                source/target, qty, references to the originating event. Immutable once
                                                                committed.

  Balance                                                       The derived (sku_id, location_id, qty_on_hand) projection from the
                                                                movement log. Refreshed after each movement commit.

  Reservation                                                   A reduction in "available qty" against a balance, held for a finalized WO that
                                                                hasn't yet completed installation. Counted as on-hand but not available for new
                                                                commitments. Released on install or WO cancellation.

  Transfer                                                      A movement with direction=TRANSFER and both source and target locations
                                                                set, recording stock leaving one location and arriving at another in a single
                                                                atomic row. Multi-SKU transfers (e.g., 20 ONTs + 15 routers shipped together)
                                                                share a batch_id across rows.

  Cycle count                                                   A physical inventory audit at a location, producing one or more ADJUSTED
                                                                movements when the counted qty differs from the system qty.

  Write-off                                                     A movement recording loss, theft, damage, expiry, or obsolescence — qty
                                                                leaves the system without going to a customer or vendor.

  Reason code                                                   An operator-extensible code categorising why a movement happened. Used for
                                                                reporting and contractor performance metrics.

  Source / target                                               For a transfer, the FROM and TO locations. For a customer install
                                                                (CONTRACTOR_VAN → customer), source is the van; target is conceptually
                                                                "the customer" but recorded as a NULL target with a wo_id reference.

 Reading the diagram:

   - Solid ■■■■■■ arrows are foreign-key references, pointing from the table that holds the FK to the table being
     referenced.

   - projects to between stock_movement and stock_balance is NOT a foreign key. stock_balance is a
     denormalised projection: qty_on_hand is recomputed asynchronously after each commit by summing the relevant
     stock_movement rows. There is no FK from balance back to a specific movement row — balance is the running total.

   - stock_reason_code is referenced by stock_movement.reason_code (FK). Both tables carry a
     movement_direction column for the same reason — it's defined on the reason code and denormalised onto the
     movement row for query convenience (so filters like "all OUTBOUND movements in May" don't need to join). Updates to
     a reason code's direction would require backfill of historical movements; v1.0 treats reason codes as effectively immutable
     once used.

   - stock_reservation.qty values flow into stock_balance.qty_reserved as an aggregate (sum of ACTIVE
     reservations for the same sku+location), not as a per-row FK.

   - The two FKs into stock_location from stock_movement (source_location_id and target_location_id) are
     shown collapsed as one arrow; in DDL they are two separate columns.

   - work_order (in WO module) is referenced by both stock_movement.wo_id (when reason is INSTALL or RETURN) and
     stock_reservation.wo_id; OSR-01 does not own this entity.

 The movement table is the only place quantity actually changes; balance + reservation are derived. A movement commit
 triggers (async) recompute of the affected (sku_id, location_id) balance. The system is eventually-consistent for balance
 reads; strictly consistent for movement reads.

 3.3 stock_movement
 The immutable ledger. Every balance change is exactly one row here.
          CREATE TABLE stock_movement (
             movement_id           TEXT PRIMARY KEY,                 -- 'mov_01HZ...' ULID
             operator_code         TEXT NOT NULL,
             sku_id                TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES equipment_sku(sku_id),

             source_location_id    TEXT NULL REFERENCES stock_location(location_id), -- where stock left from
             target_location_id    TEXT NULL REFERENCES stock_location(location_id), -- where stock arrived
             batch_id              TEXT NULL,                        -- groups multi-SKU operations (one transfer, one cycle count, on
             wo_id                 TEXT NULL,                        -- WO module reference (for INSTALL or RETURN movements)

 3.4 stock_balance
 Derived projection. Refreshed asynchronously after each movement commit.
           CREATE TABLE stock_balance (
              sku_id                TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES equipment_sku(sku_id),

 3.5 stock_reservation
 Holds committed qty per WO from WO finalization to install completion (or WO cancellation).
            CREATE TABLE stock_reservation (
               reservation_id        TEXT PRIMARY KEY,                 -- 'rsv_01HZ...' ULID
               operator_code         TEXT NOT NULL,
               sku_id                TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES equipment_sku(sku_id),
               location_id           TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES stock_location(location_id),
               qty                   INTEGER NOT NULL,
               wo_id                 TEXT NOT NULL,                    -- the WO this reservation is held for
               status                TEXT NOT NULL,                    -- 'ACTIVE' | 'CONSUMED' | 'RELEASED' | 'EXPIRED'
               expires_at            TIMESTAMP NULL,                   -- after which reservation auto-released
               consumed_at           TIMESTAMP NULL,                   -- when WO install completed and INSTALL movement was created
               released_at           TIMESTAMP NULL,                   -- when WO was cancelled and reservation was released
               consumed_movement_id TEXT NULL,                         -- the INSTALL movement that consumed this reservation
               created_at            TIMESTAMP NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(),
               created_by            TEXT NOT NULL,
               CHECK (qty > 0),
               CHECK ((status = 'CONSUMED' AND consumed_at IS NOT NULL AND consumed_movement_id IS NOT NULL) OR
                      (status = 'RELEASED' AND released_at IS NOT NULL) OR
                      (status = 'EXPIRED' AND expires_at IS NOT NULL AND consumed_at IS NULL AND released_at IS NULL) OR
                      (status = 'ACTIVE'   AND consumed_at IS NULL AND released_at IS NULL))
            );

            CREATE INDEX idx_reservation_wo                       ON stock_reservation (wo_id);
            CREATE INDEX idx_reservation_active                   ON stock_reservation (sku_id, location_id) WHERE status = 'ACTIVE';
            CREATE INDEX idx_reservation_expiring                 ON stock_reservation (expires_at) WHERE status = 'ACTIVE' AND expires_at IS NOT NUL

 Lifecycle:

   1. WO module finalizes WO → calls POST /api/stock-reservations for each equipment line → reservation row
     inserted with status=ACTIVE. stock_balance.qty_reserved += qty.

   2. Install completes → WO module calls POST /api/stock-movements with reason INSTALL referencing the WO →
     OSR-01 transitions matching reservations to status=CONSUMED, consumed_movement_id=<new movement>.
     stock_balance.qty_reserved -= qty (atomic with the movement's qty_on_hand -= qty, so qty_available
     net change is zero — the install was already accounted for via reservation).

   3. WO cancelled before install → WO module calls DELETE /api/stock-reservations/{id} → reservation
     transitions to status=RELEASED. stock_balance.qty_reserved -= qty.

   4. Expiry — a reservation with expires_at IS NOT NULL AND expires_at < NOW() and still ACTIVE is
     auto-transitioned by a periodic job (reservation-expiry-sweeper-<operator>-v<n>, every 10 min) to EXPIRED.
     The WO is flagged for review (it has no committed stock anymore).

 4. Rules
  Rule                                                       Description

  R-OSR-SC-1                                                 The movement log is immutable: stock_movement rows are append-only.
                                                             UPDATE on a movement row is blocked by trigger. To correct a wrong
                                                             movement, insert a compensating movement (e.g., a
                                                             CYCLE_COUNT_ADJUSTMENT_POS to add back qty wrongly written off). The
                                                             original row stays.

  R-OSR-SC-2                                                 Every balance change derives from movements:
                                                             stock_balance.qty_on_hand for (sku_id, location_id) = sum of all
                                                             INBOUND movements with target_location_id = location_id and
                                                             INBOUND-direction TRANSFER movements with target_location_id =
                                                             location_id, minus all OUTBOUND movements with
                                                             source_location_id = location_id and OUTBOUND-direction
                                                             TRANSFER movements with source_location_id = location_id.
                                                             Recomputation is eventually-consistent, triggered after each movement commit.

  R-OSR-SC-3                                                 TRANSFER movements have both source AND target location set. INBOUND
                                                             movements (vendor receipt, return from customer, cycle-count positive
                                                             variance) have only target set. OUTBOUND movements (install, write-off,
                                                             cycle-count negative variance) have only source set. The CHECK constraint
                                                             enforces this.

  R-OSR-SC-4                                                 Two-tier topology enforcement: TRANSFER movements MUST go from
                                                             CENTRAL_WAREHOUSE to CONTRACTOR_VAN or from CONTRACTOR_VAN to
                                                             CENTRAL_WAREHOUSE. Van-to-van transfers are NOT supported in v1.0 (would
                                                             route through central). Validated at API; future enhancement may relax.

  R-OSR-SC-5                                                 A reservation deducts from qty_available (via qty_reserved) but not from
                                                             qty_on_hand. The reserved stock is still physically at the location; it's just
                                                             committed to a specific WO. Multiple reservations can stack on the same
                                                             location until they hit qty_on_hand.

  R-OSR-SC-6                                                 Reservation consumption is atomic with the consuming movement: in the same
                                                             transaction that creates the INSTALL movement, matching reservations are
                                                             transitioned to CONSUMED. A reservation can be matched only if it (a) has the
                                                             same wo_id as the movement, (b) is currently ACTIVE, (c) covers the same
                                                             (sku_id, location_id) as the movement's source. Quantity matching is
                                                             exact — a 1-unit movement can consume exactly one 1-unit reservation.

  R-OSR-SC-7                                                 Reservation expiry: a reservation with expires_at < NOW() and still ACTIVE
                                                             is auto-expired by the periodic sweeper. Default expires_at = created_at
                                                             + 30 days if not specified by caller. Expired reservations release their
                                                             qty_reserved contribution. The associated WO is flagged for review (no
                                                             committed stock; can't install).

  R-OSR-SC-8                                                 Negative qty is forbidden: stock_balance.qty_on_hand is constrained ≥ 0.
                                                             Movements that would drive a balance negative are rejected by the API with
                                                             INSUFFICIENT_STOCK (e.g., trying to write off 5 ONTs from a van that only
                                                             has 3).

  R-OSR-SC-9                                                 Write-off and cycle-count adjustment movements require approved_by per the
                                                             reason code's requires_approval = true flag. API rejects movements
                                                             without an approver for those reason codes. v1.0 does not enforce approver
                                                             authority hierarchy (any authenticated user can approve); future enhancement
                                                             may require role check.

  R-OSR-SC-10                                                Stock query semantics: GET /api/stock-balances?location=X&sku=Y
                                                             returns the latest committed balance. Stock availability queries (GET
                                                             /api/stock-availability/{wo_context}) read qty_available which
                                                             reflects active reservations. Both are eventually-consistent; lag is typically <1s
                                                             but can be longer under high movement volume.

  R-OSR-SC-11                                                Operator-scoped queries by default: all REST endpoints scope to the
                                                             requesting actor's operator unless they hold sophix-superadmin role.
                                                             Cross-operator stock visibility is not granted to operator-local roles.

  Rule                                                              Description

  R-OSR-SC-12                                                       Balance recompute integrity: after each movement commit, a background
                                                                    worker recomputes stock_balance.qty_on_hand for the affected
                                                                    (sku_id, location_id) rows. If multiple movements commit in quick
                                                                    succession, the recompute coalesces (only the latest state is written). A nightly

 7. Stub retirement + future expansion
 OSR-01 v1.0 does not retire any existing stub from another DD. It DOES create one stub of its own and lays the foundation
 for two future DDs that will retire OSR-01-side scope.

 7.1 Stub created by this DD
 osr_contractor_stub(contractor_id PK, contractor_name) — a minimal placeholder satisfying the FK
 reference from stock_location.contractor_id (for CONTRACTOR_VAN rows). Retired when EM-02 (Contractor &
 Staff Registry) lands. The cutover pattern follows the standard: deploy EM-02; backfill contractor identities to EM-02's
 contractor table; switch the FK in stock_location.contractor_id to point at EM-02's contractor PK; drop
 osr_contractor_stub.

 7.2 Future OSR-INSTANCE-01 (or equivalent)
 When individual equipment instance tracking is needed (likely driven by RMA workflow requirements), a successor DD will
 add:

   - equipment_instance(instance_id PK, sku_id FK, serial_number, current_location_id,
     current_status) — one row per physical unit.

   - equipment_instance_lifecycle_event — append-only state transitions per instance.

   - A migration path: OSR-01's SKU+qty movement records are bulk-extended with synthetic instance_id references
     where reconstructable, or marked as unverifiable pre-OSR-INSTANCE-01.

 7.3 Future OSR-RMA-01 (or equivalent)
 RMA / repair / refurbishment workflows are out of v1.0 scope. The future DD will:

   - Orchestrate the vendor-RMA process: write-off-pending → returned-to-vendor → repaired → received-back.

   - Provide RMA tracking at the instance level (depends on OSR-INSTANCE-01).

   - Integrate with WO module for return-WO types.
