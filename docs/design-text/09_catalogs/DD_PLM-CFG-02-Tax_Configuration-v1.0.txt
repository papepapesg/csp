 A — Target
 1. What this process is about
 When BIL-01 Core processes any charge (saga intent, scheduled recurring, lifecycle event, admin action, customer
 purchase), it needs to compute tax lines on top of the discounted gross. The tax math is operator-specific and varies per
 taxable category (Internet vs TV vs Voice vs equipment fees) and per customer category (residential vs business, in some
 jurisdictions). The cascade order and base method also vary — Kenya cascades VAT over excise; other jurisdictions may
 apply taxes additively.

 PLM-CFG-02 centralizes all this:

 Inputs: operator, taxable kind + reference (what is being charged), base amount, currency, customer category, taxable-at
 date, optional override flags (rare — e.g., tax-exempt customer).

 Process:

   1. Resolve the tax group via applicability rules (Drools). One group per (operator, taxable, customer category, date).

   2. Iterate tax rules in order_within_group. For each: compute the base per base_method, apply the rate, round per the
     group's rounding mode, record the tax line.

   3. Sum tax lines, return the breakdown.

 Outputs: array of tax lines, total tax amount, total amount with tax.

 Outside this DD:

   - Tax invoice generation and regulator signing/submission — owned by BIL-02-TAX-01. PLM-CFG-02 stops at "here
     are the tax amounts for this charge." The downstream document and the eTIMS/EFD signing are TAX-01.

   - Per-charge tax ledger — the tax line breakdown is stored in BIL-01's charge_ledger.outcome_envelope JSONB
     and in BIL-02's invoice line records. PLM-CFG-02 does NOT keep its own ledger; it's a stateless compute service plus a
     config catalog.

   - Tax reporting to regulators (monthly returns, etc.) — out of scope; future reporting DD.

   - Withholding tax, customs duty, import VAT — out of scope. PLM-CFG-02 covers transaction taxes on customer
     charges only.

 B — Current TZ
 Workshop materials do not describe a centralized tax configuration service in TZ Sophix. Tax computation appears to live in
 scattered hardcoded methods across billing modules, with rates baked into code constants or per-package metadata. Tax
 invoice generation (TZ EFD integration) is handled by a separate post-payment script. No verified TZ baseline for the
 structure of the tax configuration; this DD designs the V3 target without claiming a documented TZ parallel.



 C — Gap & bridge
 PLM-CFG-02 is new functionality. It introduces:

   - Centralized tax rate catalog with operator-scoped, time-versioned rules

   - Cascading tax computation via configurable base methods (BASE / BASE_PLUS_PRIOR / BASE_PLUS_EXCISE)

   - Drools-based applicability resolution decoupling tax rules from billing code

   - Stateless compute service consumable by BIL-01, BIL-02-ADJ-01, BIL-02-GEN-01

   - Explicit regulator metadata (regulator + regulator_tax_code) enabling BIL-02-TAX-01's eTIMS/EFD/etc. submission

   - Customer-level and admin-level exemption mechanisms

   - Config change events driving cache invalidation across consumers

   - Effective-date versioning so rate changes (e.g., KE Internet excise 10% → 15% in 2023) are accurately applied to
     historical and future charges

 Cleanup work the catalog needs at operator onboarding:

   - Wananchi KE: confirmed rate set (Internet excise 15% + VAT 16%; TV VAT 16%; Voice excise 10% + VAT 16%)

   - Wananchi UG / TZ: rates to be confirmed at deployment time against URA / TRA published rate sheets

   - Yas Senegal: rates to be confirmed at deployment time against DGID published rate sheets

   - Per-operator KJAR ({OPERATOR}_applicability.drl) seeded with package-prefix dispatch rules at go-live
