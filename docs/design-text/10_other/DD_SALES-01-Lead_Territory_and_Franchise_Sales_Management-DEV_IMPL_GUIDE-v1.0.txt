 handoff to fulfillment order capture.

 3. Build Order
   1. Implement lead create/search/update.

   2. Implement franchise/territory assignment references.

   3. Implement duplicate lead/customer checks through ILM where needed.

   4. Implement activity/follow-up logging.

   5. Implement HomePass/serviceability link through RLM.

   6. Implement conversion to FUL-02 order capture.

   7. Emit lead converted/closed/activity events for REP-01 and timeline consumers.


 4. MVP Rules
   - SALES owns pre-order lead and attribution only.

   - FUL-02 owns submitted customer orders.

   - ILM owns customer master.

   - RLM owns HomePass/serviceability.

   - Advanced commissions and territory optimization are later scope.


 5. Tests
   - Lead creation and reassignment.

   - Duplicate lead/customer detection.

   - Territory/franchise scoping.

   - Lead conversion to order.

   - Sales activity timeline.

   - Reporting event emission.
